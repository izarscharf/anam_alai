## 2012 | "The legacy of the White Rose resistance movement today"
Exhibition on the occasion of the Willi Graf Memorial Day at the WGG 2011 and various exhibitions in Munich in 2012

This project has been running for over a year with a group of sixth form students as part of a P-seminar and includes the areas of drawing, painting and installation, but also interior design, urban planning concepts for the Munich area as well as the planning of various exhibitions in the school, in public spaces and on the Internet. The starting point in terms of content is a catalog of values drawn up by the pupils, which consciously sees itself as a continuation of the values of the young resistance fighters of the "White Rose" in Nazi Germany, but also refers to human rights and the struggle of young people in general for inner and outer freedom: Freedom and responsibility, critical thinking and civil courage, the courage to individuality, self-determination and self-realization, solidarity and political commitment, but also the need to recognize human dignity, the need for tolerance, intercultural understanding and diversity.

The students combine images of the resistance fighters with symbols of freedom and peace, but also with appropriate quotes from history, politics, literature, poetry and music. Inspired by expressionist prints and contemporary street art, this artistic interaction of writing and of text and image in space is intended to encourage everyone to live by the above values.

The project "The legacy of the White Rose today" will be published in the near future on the website of the "White Rose Foundation" in Munich. An exhibition of the of the pupils' works by the foundation in the form of a traveling exhibition throughout Germany is also planned. The project is also taking part in the Munich school competition for urban development.

## 2012 | "Art festival of the Munich schools"
The intensive art course Additum Kunst with students from year 12 is taking part in this year's Munich Art Festival with an assemblage/installation on the subject of "Eating and Drinking". In the installation, bourgeois eating habits and forms in our society are critically reflected and ironized. The main themes are topics such as factory farming and meat mania, eating disorders and world hunger, visions of the future of food intake and the food situation, cultural clichés and a creative approach to linguistic expressions relating to food as a starting point for artistic interpretations.

## 2011 | "Project week "A just world for all" (2500 participants)
Concept, organization and implementation of a three-day project for the whole school from 5th to 11th grade to deal theoretically and practically with the main topics of the Millennium Development Goals in various projects: Eradication of extreme poverty and hunger, sustainable management of the environment, equal rights and empowerment of women worldwide and creating a global, equitable partnership. Understanding cultures, promoting tolerance, fair trade and refugee issues as well as social commitment to marginalized groups and those in social need in our society and human rights in general were important topics in the projects designed by pupils and teachers. The results of the project work were presented in the form of films, performances, pieces of music, art installations and photographic and written documentation in an exhibition and a stage show.

## 2009 | Theatre Evening “Blindness“
- an interdisciplinary project for the upper school in the subjects of art, ethics, German, history and Spanish, to reflect on blindness not only in the literal sense, but to take the absence of sight as an opportunity to become aware of the relationship to other senses and to redefine the ability to see. In addition to aesthetic and philosophical considerations, topics such as "blind justice", personal, social and ideological blindness and blindness were examined. In the end, a theater evening was created from various plays, films and performances with self-written theater texts and their own dramaturgy
