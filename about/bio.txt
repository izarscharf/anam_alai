Anam Alai is the artist name of Uta Schärf, born in 1971 in Pretoria, South Africa.

She studied philosophy, psychology and art history in Munich before studying fine arts at Sint-Lukas in Brussels (1992–1996), followed by postgraduate studies in fine arts at the National Higher Institute for Fine Arts in Antwerp and in cultural sciences at the Free University of Brussels, where she also completed a master's in philosophy and psychology. In the 1990s she exhibited in Brussels and Flanders, including a solo exhibition at Plateau, Laureaten ’96 at the Sint-Lukasgalerij and the touring exhibitions Object-Reader and Object-Reader II.

For more than twenty years she has combined her own practice with teaching art, ethics and philosophy – at the Willi-Graf-Gymnasium in Munich, the Clay-Schule in Berlin and, since 2014, the European School Mol in Belgium. With her students she has created public art, theatre and human-rights projects, among them „Das Erbe der Weißen Rose“, shown in the Munich U-Bahn, and a refugee project in Berlin.
